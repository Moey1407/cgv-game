        Gameplay & systems

        Guard perception, patrol and search
        Social cover, badge copying and CCTV loops
        Safecracking, moving lasers and checkpoints
        Echo recording, two-actor puzzles and extraction

        World & movement

        Three authored museum layouts
        Collision, navigation, vents and rooftop routes
        Scripted zipline rig and escape camera

        Visuals & animation

        Procedural architecture and formalwear characters
        Skinned character animation and walking gala guests
        Woven carpet, marble, metal and wall materials
        Lighting, reflections and custom sound-ring shaders

        Story & presentation

        Mission guidance, chapter conversations and transitions
        Menus, accessibility options and this closing roll

        Sound & testing

        Reactive music, positional effects and character dialogue
        Gameplay tests, performance tuning and browser playtesting
