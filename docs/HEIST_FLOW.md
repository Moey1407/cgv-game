# SIGNAL: playable story and level flow

This branch uses one continuous heist night. The gala is reconnaissance. The Star is moved into the safe after closing. Wren leaves normally, then returns at 01:30 using access she prepared while the museum was open. Okafor does not know Wren; the guest-list cover protects normal behaviour, not an exposed crime or trespass.

## 1. The Gala — access and reconnaissance

1. Dex has put Wren on the guest list. She enters as a donor, with no reason for security to recognise her.
2. Watch Okafor and the crowd. His inspections create stationary opportunities along the west wall. A close badge copy takes 0.75 seconds; the original stays on his belt. No ten-second moving pickpocket is required.
3. The Grand Hall feed and nearby guests can witness a copy. Loop the feed and choose a clear inspection stop. A sustained close tail makes Okafor turn to check the guest. Suspicion follows behaviour, not a face he has never met.
4. Cross into the staff area and authorise the copied badge for the night visit at the rear reader. This books access; it does not swing the vault open during the party. Guest cover ends beyond the velvet line. The corridor cameras remain a separate risk.
5. Return to the lobby and use the public exit. The stage cannot finish just by opening the rear door. Optional evidence is the security roster identifying Dex as a consultant.

The badge can be copied during the event because Okafor is present and guest access provides cover. The staff reader authorises a later service entry. Returning at night is now the plan and the actual progression, rather than an unexplained gap.

## 2. The Vault — physical security and the objective

1. At 01:30, enter through the authorised staff entrance. The gala is over. The Star is now in the safe. The badge has completed its purpose; it does not grant public guest cover after hours.
2. Learn the high beam by crawling, then the low beam by jumping. The moving beam must be observed. Lasers have physical housings and movement rails. Collision checks the entire body rather than two isolated sample heights.
3. Choose the blinking inner checkpoint or the slower maintenance duct. The duct begins beyond the first three teaching beams, so it does not skip the entire lesson. It is a visible corridor with W/S crawling, not a fade-and-teleport shortcut.
4. Reach the west service panel while Briggs patrols. Hold the control to isolate the safe's tamper circuit. The safe cannot be used before this; the panel has a clear security purpose.
5. Crack three tumblers. The near-contact sound is a real double click; fast turning carries noise to Briggs. Archive crates offer physical cover and affect line of sight and routes.
6. Open the safe, explicitly take the Kestrel Star, then return to the marked service fire door. Opening the safe does not automatically collect the stone or finish the level.

There are no unrelated watch, purse, sketch or deposit-box collectibles. The Star is the sole financial target; the three optional files change the ending.

## 3. The Double-Cross — use what you learned to escape

1. The service door leads into the lockdown wing. Dex reveals the insurance arrangement: Wren is meant to be the culprit. The rig and the roof line are Wren's own fallback, established earlier in the story.
2. The two-operator security system is a signal/occupancy interlock. An Echo replays the rig's recorded control signal; the fiction does not require a weightless hologram to physically press a heavy button.
3. Record holding the shutter override, rewind, and cross while the Echo repeats the input.
4. Two patrols search the gallery. Use cover, a decoy, the Echo or the maintenance duct to reach the north-east stairwell. This duct avoids part of the patrol route, not either required interlock.
5. On the landing, put Wren and the replay on separate occupancy sensors. The two-operator condition unlocks the roof override; hold it to release the hatch.
6. If all three evidence files were found, the roof override transmits them. The exposed-Dex ending now has an explicit cause during play.
7. Take the pre-rigged zipline. The first-person mount, sleeves, gloves, trolley, acceleration, lean, cable sound and service-roof landing form the escape. The building timer stops once Wren is safely on the line.

The escape timer is six minutes in this revision to accommodate the added route and first-time Echo learning. Treat this as a tuning value, not a proven final target. Difficulty comes from observation, witnesses, patrols and combining tools; it should not come from undisclosed rules or a need to hurry through captions.

## Transitions and teaching

The opening introduces the backstory and guest-list setup. Ordinary level changes now preserve the mouse lock and use a short scene fade with a small time/location caption. They no longer chain a story page into a full briefing page. If the mouse has been released, the browser still needs a single arrival click to lock it again. Full controls are optional and remain accessible from Pause → Controls; nearby actions and brief hints teach the relevant input in the world.

Long final voice performances extend their captions and opening shots instead of being cut off by a fixed reading timer. Rewritten lines cannot play an old recording with a contradictory script. Missing approved performances remain captioned.

## Design references

[HITMAN's freedom of approach](https://ioi.dk/hitman/game-features) informs the public cover, observation windows and reactions to behaviour. [Dishonored's themed missions and different routes](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/dishonored-2-inside-the-epic-themed-missions) inform the distinct purpose of each stage and the slow/quiet versus direct/risky route choice. These are design references, not claims that SIGNAL has their production scope or finished asset quality.
